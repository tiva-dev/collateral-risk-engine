| ADR-008 | 2026-11-25 | **One liquidation planner** (`app/liquidation/planning.py`) for the evaluator, monitoring and replay. | ADR-009 | 2026-11-26 | **Market-agnostic mathematics, asset-specific intelligence, market-aware execution.** CRI has one universal risk and recovery framework. A market is never given its own mathematical model. The architecture has five separate layers. (1) Asset statistics are estimated automatically from observed data: volatility, tails, gaps, spread and liquidity. Instruments with short histories use hierarchical (empirical-Bayes) shrinkage, which can borrow information across markets (`app/markets/hierarchical.py`). (2) Portfolio risk covers dependence, concentration and currency exposure. (3) Exchange and execution characteristics are data, not code, and each field records its provenance (`app/markets/profiles.py`): calendar, settlement, price limits, participation and convertibility. (4) A universal capacity and recovery framework. (5) Institutional risk appetite and policy controls, held separately. A country-specific LTV ceiling is a temporary safeguard or a client policy constraint. It does not replace the methodology. Each new market is onboarded through a standard validation (`app/markets/onboarding.py`). Generalization is tested by withholding whole markets and instrument groups (`research/methodology_lab/generalization.py`), not only by within-market validation. Recording NGX price limits in a profile does not approve price-limit modelling (unchanged from the original scope). | accepted as architecture principle (Tiva directive) | Tiva; engineering | `research/methodology_lab/generalization.py` results; `/admin/markets/onboarding` |
